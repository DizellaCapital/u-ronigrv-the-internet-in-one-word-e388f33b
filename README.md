The entry is exactly one word: **Captcha** — the whole internet in one word. Every human on earth has been stopped by a little box and asked to prove, to a machine, that they are not a machine. It is funny on its own (the robot interrogating the human about being human), true of the internet as a whole rather than any one site or app, surprising (nobody's first reach is a gatekeeper), and at seven bytes it lands in one beat.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only) writes the word to `entry.txt` in `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (fallback `/output`), validates it (exactly one word, 1–30 characters, not only whitespace), and verifies it by read-back.
